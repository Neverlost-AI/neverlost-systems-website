The communication gap

Communicating complex needs takes time and energy.

Patients need to explain changing symptoms, how daily life is affected, and what help they need. Fatigue, brain fog, and limited appointment time can make that difficult.

Healthcare professionals need clear, relevant information to understand those experiences and determine how to help.

Our guiding question

What work can we take off the patient’s plate?

We’re building Neverlost to reduce how much patients have to organize, remember, and reconstruct as they move through care.

The initial product

Making that communication easier

Neverlost OS — In Development

One patient-controlled context layer.

Neverlost OS is the shared infrastructure we’re developing to carry important context between care interactions. The goal is to keep information connected to its sources, preserve uncertainty and corrections, and support patient control over what gets shared.

About

Why I built Neverlost

Jeff Summerhays, Founder

I started Neverlost after seeing how much work it took to communicate my own complex health needs. Even with a knowledgeable care team, I still had to bring information together and explain what was happening before others could help.

I began documenting what helped: organizing records, preparing for appointments, and connecting my experiences to the questions my providers needed to answer.

That process became the starting point for Neverlost. I’m building software to make that preparation and communication easier for other patients and the people supporting them.
