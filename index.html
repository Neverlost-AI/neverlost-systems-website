The appointment story

Two perspectives. One appointment.

You may be going to a yearly checkup, an eye appointment, or a specialist visit. You bring a history of experiences. The person caring for you needs the parts that matter for this conversation.

Illustrative perspectives

The patient

“I need you to understand what’s been happening, what I’ve tried, and how it affects my life.”

The healthcare professional

“I need the relevant history, what has changed, and the questions we need to work through together.”

Both want a useful conversation. Too much of the appointment can be spent reconstructing the story before that conversation begins.

Our guiding question

What work can we take off the patient’s plate?

Life continues between appointments. Neverlost Healthcare is being built to help those everyday experiences and important decisions carry forward, so preparation can start with the context already gathered.

One connected product

From everyday experience to the next conversation.

Neverlost Healthcare connects capture, history, review, appointment preparation, and follow-up in one patient-side application. Our working prototype brings these steps together.

  1. 01

    Record your experience

    Capture changes, concerns, and questions when you have the capacity.

  2. 02

    Bring history together

    Find relevant records and experiences, with their original sources available.

  3. 03

    Review what changed

    Check new or changed information, correct proposed wording, and choose what belongs.

  4. 04

    Prepare both reports

    Review talking points for you and relevant, source-linked context for your provider.

  5. 05

    Carry it into follow-up

    Keep updates, unresolved questions, and next steps connected to the continuing history.

First conversation

Make room for what matters now.

Alex brings everyday experiences, earlier records, and questions into preparation for a primary care visit. Alex reviews the proposed wording before deciding what to include.

For Alex

Manageable talking points

  • What has been happening?
  • How is daily life affected?
  • What do I want help understanding?

For the provider

Relevant context and its sources

  • Selected patient accounts and earlier records
  • Dates, attribution, and supporting passages
  • Open questions kept visible

What carries forward: the reviewed history, original sources, and questions that still need attention.

A different conversation

Prepare for the specialist without starting over.

For neurology, Alex can bring earlier context together with more recent experiences. What belongs in this specialist conversation is reviewed for that purpose.

For Alex

Questions for this visit

  • What has changed since primary care?
  • What have I already discussed or tried?
  • Which questions remain unresolved?

For the provider

Specialist-relevant preparation

  • Earlier context alongside newer information
  • Source-linked accounts and records
  • Uncertainty and corrections retained

What carries forward: the specialist record and subsequent experiences can become part of the next preparation after capture and review.

Return to primary care

Begin with what happened next.

At PCP follow-up, Alex prepares an updated view of the continuing story. New information can change the current draft while earlier decisions and saved report versions remain available.

For Alex

A clearer follow-up conversation

  • What happened after the specialist visit?
  • What was manageable, difficult, or still unclear?
  • What should we discuss next?

For the provider

Updates in context

  • The specialist record and newer patient accounts
  • Changes tied to their supporting sources
  • Unresolved questions and next steps

What carries forward: a more useful starting point for the next conversation, with the deeper history still available.

Why I built Neverlost

“What would it look like if the context carried forward?”

Jeff Summerhays, Founder

I began with a question: What happened to me? As my health situation became more complicated, more people began holding different pieces of the story.

Every new provider, application, insurance question, or major decision could mean reconstructing that story again. Even with a knowledgeable care team, bringing the relevant pieces together still took work.

The question became larger than my own case. Neverlost Healthcare is my effort to help patients carry that context forward and prepare for a more useful conversation with the people caring for them.
