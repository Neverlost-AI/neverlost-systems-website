The encounter problem

Two perspectives. One 20-minute visit.

The patient is thinking

“I need you to understand two years of problems, symptoms, changes, and what I’ve already tried.”

The provider is thinking

“I need to understand two years of problems, symptoms, changes, and what’s already been done.”

All in a 20-minute visit.

That can create pain on both sides.

Neither side is the problem.

Neverlost is built to carry the larger story forward, prepare what matters for the encounter in front of them, and keep the deeper context available when it is needed.

Patient advocacy — Initial focus

A patient should not have to walk into an important appointment hoping they remember everything that matters.

Neverlost’s patient-advocacy model is persistent memory prepared before the visit. Less rushing to reconstruct the story, less forgetting what matters, and more continuity from one appointment to the next.

About

Why I built Neverlost

I began with a question:

What happened to me?

As my own health situation became more complicated, I spent years trying to understand it. Eventually, more people began holding different pieces of the answer.

The problem changed.

Every new provider, system, application, insurance question, or major decision could require some version of the story to be reconstructed again.

The question became larger than my own case:

What would it look like if the context carried forward?

Neverlost is where I started trying to build an answer to that problem.
