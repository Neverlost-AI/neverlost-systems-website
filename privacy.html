Website privacy

Updated November 10, 2026

This notice covers the public Neverlost Systems website, its Neverlost Healthcare waitlist, and clinic inquiries. The waitlist is not a patient account or a place to submit medical records.

Waitlist details

The signup form asks for your name and email address. We use those details to send Neverlost Healthcare development updates and pilot participation opportunities. Joining does not provide product access or guarantee enrollment.

The form sends your submission through FormSubmit to jeff@neverlostsystems.com. FormSubmit processes the submission as a separate service and may display spam-prevention or confirmation steps.

Clinic inquiries

The “Discuss a pilot” action opens an email to Jeff. Your email provider handles sending it. We use the details you choose to include to respond and discuss a possible evaluation. Please share practice contact information and a general description of the workflow, without patient records or identifying health information.

Website activity

The website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and navigation, including which sections people view. Google Analytics can use cookies and browser or device information. See Google’s explanation of Analytics data and its browser opt-out add-on.

The site’s custom navigation events do not include the name or email entered in the waitlist form. Other websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices.

Updates, corrections, and removal requests

Contact jeff@neverlostsystems.com to stop updates, correct your contact details, or request removal from the waitlist. Signup and inquiry details may also be present in email correspondence; tell us which request or address you want us to review.

Patient information stays separate

A website signup is not permission to access a patient’s private information. Proposed practice sponsorship does not automatically authorize access to patient records. Any product pilot requires its own participation and information-sharing arrangements.
